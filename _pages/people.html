Matt Childers, Ph.D.

Acting Assistant Professor · Department of Bioengineering · University of Washington, Seattle

Our research pairs structure-based atomic- and molecular-scale simulations with kinetic assays to model complex biomolecular assemblies and to engineer muscle contraction.

I completed my B.S. of Biomedical Engineering at the University of Virginia (2013), my Ph.D. in Bioengineering at the University of Washington (2019), and my postdoctoral training at the University of Washington (2024).
