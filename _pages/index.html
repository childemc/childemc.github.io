Goals:

The Childers Group at the University of Washington seeks to understand the molecular mechanisms through which complex assemblies in the sarcomere generate force in healthy and diseased states. Using atomic- and molecular-scale simulations, kinetic assays, and stem-cell derived cardiac cells, we study the relationships between structure, dynamics, and function. Ultimately, we aim to uncover the principles governing muscle function and design molecules that can modulate it.
